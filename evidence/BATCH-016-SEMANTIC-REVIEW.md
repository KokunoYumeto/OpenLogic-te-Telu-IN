  Reverse paraphrase: quantifiers bind the occurrences in their scope, and a

- OLP-0158 defines recursive substitution in terms and formulas, including
  the capture-avoidance condition. Reverse paraphrase: replacing a variable

  variable of the inserted term would become bound.
