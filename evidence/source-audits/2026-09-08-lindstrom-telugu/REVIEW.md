Scope: the five reader-reachable frozen units OLP-0203--OLP-0207 at

The pristine English files under \`upstream/\` and the frozen source manifest
are authoritative. This is a bounded same-agent source and translation
review, not an independent certification. Full source reading found twenty-two
local defects. The Telugu target restores the full predicate-and-constant
vocabulary of ordinary first-order sentences; repairs a wrong renaming referent; makes
relativization use the supplied relation and states when its fibre is a
substructure; restores the finite-vocabulary membership required by the
Expansion Property; makes the atomic-sentence and quantifier-language clauses
well typed; uses one shared pair of sequence-coding predicates; replaces
improper infinite Boolean constructions by finite representative choices;
prepares disjoint tagged sorts; codes the selected family of partial
isomorphisms; restores the dedicated empty-sequence notation; removes two
ambient-structure symbol collisions; distinguishes an abstract L-sentence
from a first-order coding sentence; supplies the normality assumptions used
by the finite-rank lemma and Lindström theorem; removes one bound-variable
collision and two malformed subscripts; makes the finite-vocabulary
reduction explicit; prepares compatible copies before taking structure
unions; restores abstract satisfaction subscripts; and adds the omitted
fresh-constant type that actually forces a nonstandard index. Every repair
will be disclosed next to the affected passage and recorded in the
correction ledger; no upstream byte is changed.

The chapter driver, definitions, both theorem proofs, figure and caption,
formulas, labels, references, and token identities were read across source
and target. The consulted native witnesses support the established Telugu
register for proof, theorem, formal and first-order logic, predicates,
quantifiers, sentences, structures, domains, relations, finiteness,
equivalence, and consequence. They do not directly attest abstract logic,
normal logic, expressive strength, relativization, Lindström's theorem, or
the named closure properties. Batch terminology therefore remains
definition- and formula-controlled; the eponym and specialist compounds are
not represented as canon-attested Telugu nomenclature.
