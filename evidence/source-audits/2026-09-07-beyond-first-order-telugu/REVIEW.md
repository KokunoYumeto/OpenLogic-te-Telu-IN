The pristine English files under `upstream/` and the frozen source manifest
are authoritative. This is a bounded same-agent source and translation
review, not an independent certification. Full source/target reading found
four local notation or typing defects. The Telugu target repairs the malformed
inner quantifier in the many-sorted example, restores the atomic-formula

successor notation in the arithmetic axioms, and states the lambda-bound
variable's type consistently with its formation rule. Each repair is
disclosed next to the affected passage and recorded in the correction ledger;
no upstream byte is changed.

The chapter driver and its discussions of many-sorted, second-order,
higher-order, intuitionistic and modal logics, as well as the closing survey
of other logics, were read in both languages. The relevant canon passages
support the established Telugu register for domains, variables, predicates,
formulas, derivations, identity, structure, elements, functions, sets, truth,
necessity and mathematical explanation. They do not directly attest every
specialized headword in this survey. Batch term decisions therefore make the
many-sorted, higher-order, intuitionistic, modal, accessibility and
extensional/intensional choices explicit, including grounded borrowings where
that is more precise than an unsupported coinage.
