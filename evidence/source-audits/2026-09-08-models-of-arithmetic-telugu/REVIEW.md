# Models of Arithmetic source audit for the Telugu edition

Audit ID: `OLTEMODARI-20260908`.

Scope: the seven reader-reachable frozen units OLP-0191--OLP-0197 at
OpenLogic revision `9620cc73f9c8e0ad003c514a5d3748f29611c4c0`.

The pristine English files under `upstream/` and the frozen source manifest
are authoritative. This is a bounded same-agent source and translation
review, not an independent certification. Full source reading found fifteen
local defects. The Telugu target gives the string model well-typed operation
arguments and its omitted order relation; restores a dropped proof-predicate
argument; corrects a domain/range word swap and a new-constant structure
index; covers the empty finite constraint set and supplies the missing
Downward Lowenheim--Skolem step; repairs two wrong variables in Q-model case
calculations; excludes zero from the predecessor claim; restores the defined
model-addition notation; restricts block denumerability to countable models;
repairs a set-builder variable; states Tennenbaum's theorem up to
isomorphism; and replaces a non-surjective transport map by the bijection
that generates the displayed transported operations. Every repair is
disclosed next to the affected passage and recorded in the correction
ledger; no upstream byte is changed.

The chapter driver, all definitions, proofs, exercises, formulas, tables,
labels, references, and token identities were read across source and target.
The consulted native witnesses support the established Telugu register for
natural numbers, functions, domains, finite and countable sets, first-order
logic, sentences, proof, consistency, models, and induction. They do not
directly attest standard/non-standard arithmetic models, blocks, computable
presentations, Robinson arithmetic, Peano arithmetic, true arithmetic, or
Tennenbaum's theorem. Batch terminology therefore remains definition- and
formula-controlled, with eponyms and imported abbreviations identified
explicitly rather than presented as canon-attested Telugu nomenclature.
