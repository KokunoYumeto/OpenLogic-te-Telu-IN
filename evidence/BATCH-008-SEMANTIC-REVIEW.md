## Source-aligned checks and reverse paraphrases

- OLP-0055 preserves the Propositional Logic part identity and the conditional
  import topology. It imports Syntax and Semantics, Natural Deduction and
  Other Deductive Systems unconditionally; the Proof Theory, Soundness and
  Completeness, Resolution and Normal Forms chapters remain guarded by the
  same tags. When the first-order-logic flag is false, the source's deliberate
  reuse of proof-system material from the first-order-logic tree is retained
  through the exact import paths. Reverse paraphrase: this is a structural
  part driver, not an added mathematical exposition, and no imported chapter
  has been duplicated or silently omitted.
- OLP-0056 retains the chapter title, exact five-section import sequence and
  quick-summary editorial. Reverse paraphrase: the chapter first motivates
  propositional syntax and semantics, then defines formulas, establishes
  syntactic preliminaries and formation sequences, defines valuations and
  satisfaction, and finally develops semantic notions.
- OLP-0057 preserves the motivating idea that propositional logic studies how
  truth values of compound sentences depend on atomic sentences and
  truth-functional connectives. It retains the syntax/semantics distinction,
  the promise of unique readability, and the transition from valuations and
  satisfaction to tautology, entailment, satisfiability and related notions.
  Reverse paraphrase: syntax determines which symbol strings are formulas and
  how they are constructed, whereas semantics assigns truth values and uses
  those assignments to define the central consequence notions.
- OLP-0058 preserves the definition of a propositional language by a stock of
  propositional variables and a selected signature of connectives. Every
  optional tag, alternative symbol and inactive branch remains in its source
  position. The inductive formation clauses still say that atomic variables
  are formulas and that the enabled negation, binary connective and constant
  constructions produce formulas; nothing else does. Defined connectives,
  alternative notations, syntactic identity and the distinction between an
  expression and the formula it denotes are retained. Reverse paraphrase:
  formulas are finite symbol strings generated by the displayed clauses, and
  syntactic identity is literal identity of those strings, not semantic
  equivalence. OLTEPLSYN-001 repairs the malformed closing braces and empty
  false arms in the nested defined-symbol tag test. OLTEPLSYN-002 removes the
  unmatched closing parenthesis from the abbreviation of the material
  conditional, leaving exactly “not A or B.”
- OLP-0059 retains structural induction on formulas, balanced-parenthesis and
  initial-segment results, the claim that no formula is a proper initial
  segment of another, and the unique-readability argument. It also preserves
  uniform substitution, including the distinction between replacing all
  occurrences of variables according to one assignment and merely changing a
  particular occurrence, together with the associated problems. Reverse
  paraphrase: because each non-atomic formula has one recoverable outermost
  construction, recursive definitions and inductive proofs on formulas are
  well founded and substitutions are determined uniformly by their values on
  propositional variables.
- OLP-0060 preserves both directions of the equivalence between the inductive
  definition of formulas and the formation-sequence characterization. A
  formation sequence still requires each entry to be atomic or to result from
  earlier entries by an allowed connective operation, and a formula is the
  final member of such a sequence. The proof by strong induction on the
  sequence length and the converse construction from immediate subformulas
  retain their logical dependencies. Reverse paraphrase: a string is a
  formula exactly when some finite, locally justified construction history
  ends with it. OLTEPLSYN-003 uses syntactic identity rather than semantic
  equivalence when the conjunction case identifies the current symbol string
  with the conjunction built from earlier entries.
- OLP-0061 preserves valuations as assignments of truth values to
  propositional variables and evaluations as their unique recursive
  extensions to all formulas. The truth clauses and truth tables for the
  enabled constants and connectives remain exact, as does the ternary
  truth-function problem. Local Determination still states that the value of a
  fixed formula depends only on the variables occurring in that formula. The
  satisfaction notation and its equivalence with evaluation to true are
  retained. Reverse paraphrase: once the atomic values are fixed, every
  compound formula has exactly one value obtained by its main connective; two
  valuations agreeing on the variables in A therefore agree on A.
  OLTEPLSYN-004 restores that fixed-A scope by saying “the formula A,” not the
  source's potentially existential “some formula A.”
- OLP-0062 preserves the definitions of tautology, contradiction,
  satisfiability, unsatisfiability, contingency and semantic entailment,
  including their model-theoretic quantifier directions. The propositions
  relating these notions, the characterization of an unsatisfiable set by
  entailment of a contradiction, and the Semantic Deduction Theorem retain
  their hypotheses and conclusions. Reverse paraphrase: a set entails A when
  every valuation satisfying every member of the set also satisfies A; the
  deduction theorem internalizes the addition of one premise as a material
  conditional. The Telugu wording keeps this direction distinct from the
  converse relation.

## Canon use and limits

truth/falsity register. TE-P003 and TE-P004 support reasoning, induction,
theorem and proof language. TE-P014 supports the broad function register used
for assignments and extensions. None of these pages independently attests the
selected headwords for propositional variables, logical connectives,
syntactic identity, formation sequences, valuations, satisfaction, tautology,
entailment, satisfiability, Local Determination or the Semantic Deduction
Theorem. TE-T032 and TE-T033 therefore record those grouped choices as
provisional, with their formal senses fixed by the adjacent OpenLogic
definitions, clauses, truth tables and formulas rather than overstated witness
authority.
