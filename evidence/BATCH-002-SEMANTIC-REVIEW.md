- OLP-0015: equivalence classes, quotient as the set of classes, both directions of the class-equality proof, remainder examples and exercise retained. - OLP-0017: graph specifies vertices as well as edge pairs, so isolated vertices distinguish the two diagrams. Both TikZ diagrams preserved exactly; translated intertext retains both formulas. Directed edges, self-loops and differing literature conventions retained.
- OLP-0018: rooted well-ordered predecessor sets, immediate successor/predecessor, at-most-one parent (not exactly one for every infinite-tree node), finite branching versus finite tree, maximal-chain branch, both sequence-tree examples and both forms of König's lemma retained. - R-plus means reflexive closure in Orders but transitive closure in Operations, with local definitions. The source overload is preserved.
