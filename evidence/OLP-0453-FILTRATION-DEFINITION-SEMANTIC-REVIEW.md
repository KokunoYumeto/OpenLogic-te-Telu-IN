## Reverse reading

1. The definition does not select a unique filtration. For a fixed $M$ and subformula-closed $\Gamma$, all filtrations have quotient worlds $W^*=\{[w]:w\in W\}$ and valuation $V^*(p)=\{[u]:u\in V(p)\}$, but may differ in $R^*$. `వడపోత` is now checked against this full definition and retained as a transparent, definition-controlled technical rendering; the native witnesses do not supply the modal term itself.
2. Condition R1 sends every original edge $Ruv$ to a quotient edge $R^*[u][v]$. The guarded R2 says a quotient edge plus $\Box !A\in\Gamma$ and $M,u\models\Box !A$ entails $M,v\models !A$. Guarded R3 says a quotient edge plus $\Diamond !A\in\Gamma$ and $M,v\models !A$ entails $M,u\models\Diamond !A$. The Telugu retains the distinct antecedent and consequent worlds and all tags/labels.
3. The valuation caveat is essential: $w\in V(p)$ implies $[w]\in V^*(p)$, but the converse gives only *some* $u\in V(p)$ with $[w]=[u]$, hence $w\equiv u$. It gives $w\in V(p)$ only when $p\in\Gamma$, as used in the theorem's atomic case. The Telugu does not collapse this distinction.
4. The theorem states truth preservation only for $!A\in\Gamma$ and each $w\in W$. Subformula closure licenses the induction hypothesis on every immediate subformula. The false/true constant, atomic, negation, conjunction, disjunction, conditional, biconditional, Box and Diamond cases retain their original branch tags and exercise alternatives. The negated cases in the conditional and biconditional follow by complementing the positive induction equivalences; no stronger induction assumption is asserted.
5. In the Box forward direction, R2 carries truth from $\Box !B$ at $w$ to $!B$ at an arbitrary quotient successor representative $v$, then induction transfers it to $M^*$; in the reverse direction, R1 carries any original edge to the quotient, and induction transfers $!B$ back. In the Diamond forward direction, R1 carries the original witness edge forward; in the reverse direction, R3 recovers Diamond truth in the original model from a quotient witness. The Telugu keeps these four directions separate.

The corollary transfers preservation to truth in a model and to validity in a model class; the second clause is read over the class of all $\Gamma$-filtrations of models in the given class. The source has not yet constructed particular filtration relations here; their existence/properties are subsequent work, so this review does not treat the corollary as a proved existence theorem.
