Same-agent reverse paraphrase: a reflexive accessibility relation makes `□A → A` true at every world for any formula `A`. The converse from truth under one fixed valuation is false: in a one-world, non-reflexive model with `p` true, `□p → p` is true. To recover reflexivity, require `□p → p` for **every valuation** on the same pair of worlds and relation. If reflexivity fails at `w`, choose `V(p)=W\{w}`; all accessible worlds satisfy `p` but `w` does not, so the implication fails at `w`. This motivates a frame as the ordered pair `(W,R)`, a model as a valuation-bearing structure based on that frame, and validity on a frame as truth in every based model. Thus the displayed formula characterizes reflexive frames. The translation retains the source's distinction among one formula instance, a schema, a model and a frame.
